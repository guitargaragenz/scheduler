## Three reporting bugs found and fixed

All three made the numbers look **worse** than reality, and all three were found only
because Trevor rejected the reports rather than accepting them. That is worth noting for
future meetings: the reports were confidently wrong, not obviously broken.

1. **Parts count always read zero.** The filter matched `action === 'PARTS'`, a string that
   never existed in `ACTION_OPTIONS`. Fixed by introducing `WP` and filtering on it.
2. **Finance always read $0.** It filtered completed jobs on the Monday of the week being
   *planned* — a week that hasn't happened. Now reports the week just worked.
3. **GST applied backwards.** `invoice_amount` is stored **ex GST** (Trevor: "all figures I
   provide with revenue will be the same"), but the report divided by 1.15 as though it were
   inclusive, understating revenue by 13% in every report ever run.

Fixed in commit `76b3582`.
