SVT-6 PRO — wiring map for reassembly

Every lead and plug on the power amp board, in the order you'll land them. Taken from the factory final-assembly drawing (00-541-XX rev 5, sheet 3), the power amp pictorial (07-699 rev B) and schematic 07S699.

1Check first: which mains version is this amp?

The transformer leads land on different tabs for each mains voltage. Wrong version = wrong voltage on the transformer the moment it's powered.

Your amp will be one of the two export versions, U or W. They differ by one lead. To tell which:

  • One primary lead is not used and sits cable-tied to the other primary wires. That tie stays on even with the leads unplugged.
  • BLK/WHT tied off → U version.   BLACK tied off → W version.
  • Cross-check: the small in-line part (item 74) plugs onto whichever of those two is used.

If nothing is tied off and both BLACK and BLK/WHT have tabs to go to, stop. That's the US 120 V (D) or Japan 100 V (J) wiring. It must not go on NZ mains.

Item 74 sits in line between J21 and the lead: its female end goes on the J21 tab, its male end takes the Black (U) or Black/White (W) lead. Place it so it isn't touching any wiring.

Tabs J20 and J22 stay empty on both export versions. They're for the 120 V / 100 V wiring.

Convenience outlet

US models only, so there's none on this export amp. Its tabs (J16, and J17 or J18) stay empty.

The drawings disagree on the neutral tab. The schematic says the neutral goes to the switch from J17; the assembly drawing says J18. Put it back on whichever tab it came off. The board's printed labels settle it. Without the outlet, it's the only lead going to that pair.

7Wire dressing — factory notes

  • Tie the unused primary lead (from section 1) back to the other primary wires.
  • Bundle W5, W6, W4 with the fan wires. Tie about 65 mm (2½ in) from the solder end.
  • Bundle W1–W6 together at C21 and tie.
  • Twist the yellow and white input wires (W8, W11) together.
  • Twist the J9 ribbon twice over the lead beside it (item 55), which keeps the ribbon up off the board.
  • Fan wires twisted, leaving by the top notch of the fan brace.
  • Transformer turned for least hum. Put it back the way it came out.
